---
name: Global cash transfers
description: Cross-store visibility and authorization for transfers between employee caisses
---

Money transfers involving an employee's personal caisse must remain visible and actionable from every store that employee can access in the same ERP company.

**Why:** The user reported that employees had to switch back to the sending store to see a cash transfer. Personal caisses are company-wide, so the original store must not determine transfer access.

**How to apply:** Preserve the original store for accounting history, but authorize transfer visibility by caisse participation and decisions by sender/recipient ownership. Keep company isolation and current-store access validation. This rule does not widen access to colleagues' transfers or change product-transfer workflows.
