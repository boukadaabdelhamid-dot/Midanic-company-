name: Legacy sales-source integrity
description: Historical ERP sales channels can be misclassified; distinguish stored labels from evidence of origin.
---

The user confirmed that several actual quick sales appear as “En ligne”. Treat historical channel labels as potentially unreliable, not as proof that a storefront checkout occurred. A missing seller alone does not establish that a sale was online.

**Why:** Legacy inference of sale channels can misclassify genuine counter sales. Fixing new-sale classification does not establish the true origin of already-labelled records.

**How to apply:** Verify historical records individually before proposing corrections. The user approved leaving old records unchanged during the sales-source separation work; obtain explicit authorization before changing them, and keep any source correction separate from stock, payment, and balance operations.
